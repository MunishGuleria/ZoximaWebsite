# Brand marks

Drop approved SVG files here and they render automatically in place of the
typeset wordmark. File name = slug used in the component, e.g. `salesforce.svg`.

## Where to get files with usage rights
- **Salesforce partner badge** — Salesforce Partner Community → Partner Marketing Center.
- **Microsoft partner badge** — Microsoft Partner Center → Marketing → Logo builder.
- **Client logos** — written marketing consent from each client; they supply the file.
- **OpenAI / Anthropic / SAP / LangChain** — each publishes brand guidelines; request
  "built with" assets rather than lifting corporate logos.

Do not source logos from Google Images. Logo use (unlike plain-text naming)
implies endorsement under both US and Indian trademark law and needs permission.
